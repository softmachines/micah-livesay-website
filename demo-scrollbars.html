// SCROLLBAR STYLE DEMOS

JavaScript custom scrollbars — works in all browsers. Scroll inside each panel.

1 — Ghost Line
[ PROFILE.SYS ]

Ultra-minimal 2px glowing line. Near invisible — presence hinted rather than shown.

Works best for content-heavy pages where UI should disappear. Keeps full terminal immersion.

The faint phosphor glow is the only visual indicator of scroll position.

2 — Segmented
[ PROFILE.SYS ]

Dashed thumb in a dark channel. Looks like a tape counter or old-school progress indicator.

The gaps between dashes add rhythm. Strongly terminal — feels like a text-mode UI status bar.

Good contrast between track and thumb without demanding attention.

3 — Channel Rail
[ PROFILE.SYS ]

Double-border track with inset thumb. The thumb rides a physical channel — like a mixing console fader.

Mechanical and precise. The border treatment gives clear bounds to the scroll zone.

Thicker than the ghost line but still refined. Works well for content-heavy pages.

4 — Neon Blade CREATIVE
[ PROFILE.SYS ]

No track — just a blade of light. Bright centre diffuses to transparent edges, floating in space.

Pulses slowly like a phosphor signal. Animated glow breathes on a 2.5s loop.

Feels like a HUD element from a sci-fi interface. The scrollbar appears to hover.

5 — CA Glitch CREATIVE
[ PROFILE.SYS ]

Chromatic aberration on the thumb — pink and cyan shadows alternate continuously, matching the site's glitch language.

Tick-mark track looks like a ruler or tape transport. Thumb appears to glitch left-right as CA cycles.

Most distinctive — immediately part of the same visual system as the site's other effects.
